About

If you asked me "tell me about yourself"

My name is James Praise. I say a lot of things on the internet under the moniker "Realjaymes." My newsletters and bite-sized videos are themed RealTalk.

What's real? What are we talking about? And most importantly, who is James?

There's been a decades-long identity crisis among people who play James Bond. Sean Connery, Roger Moore, Pierce Brosnan, Daniel Craig, and a few others have all made a case for who really embodies James, or Bond, or James Bond. My take: all of them have been as real or authentic as whoever is currently playing Ian Fleming's fictional MI6 agent.

What's real, and what are we talking about?

I'm sharing everything I know about marketing, business, and building wealth: templates, AI-assisted tools, mental models, frameworks, and systems I actually use to earn more, build things that make money, and grow what I keep.

I've generally found that the fastest way to get things done is with templates based on real frameworks, so I create and share templates other people can use for similar problems, in life and in business.

I'm not a guru or an expert with all the answers. I'm a friend and a brother who wants to help. I share fundamentals that have helped me make sense of systems in life, and I'm doing this partly to give my kids leverage, so they can be better than me when they grow up.

Who is James?

A lot of well-known men have carried the first name James. None of them carried a last name that turns into an instruction if you read it backward. I do. My name is James Praise.

I may have done one or two things worth praising. But that also raises the question: why should you praise James by default? So here I am, on a path to actually be worthy of it.

At the moment, I build, I write, I speak, I teach, and I mentor. These are the channels I use to add value. I'm also trying to democratize common sense by writing, speaking, and teaching it.

My name is James Praise. I'm not perfect. But I'm real, and I have ideas worth sharing.

The background version, if you want fewer jokes

Over the past 7+ years, I've worked with early to growth stage companies across three continents (Africa, North America, and Europe), leading go-to-market strategy, customer acquisition, and demand generation.

I've partnered with bootstrapped, seed, and Series A startups across B2B, SaaS, Fintech, HRTech, EdTech, HealthTech, and MarTech. I've been a founding marketer multiple times, building marketing functions from the ground up at Reform, FunnelEnvy, Bujeti, Distrobird, and Cecure Intelligence, while mentoring founders through SBC Afritech and PAADC accelerators.

I'm the founder of Marketing In Action, an execution-first marketing ecosystem for founders and marketers, and a cofounder and consultant at Titaja, a GTM services agency for product-led companies.
