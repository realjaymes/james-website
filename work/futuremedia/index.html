You're hiring a Head of PPC, a Senior PPC Manager and a Head of Growth at the same time, so how the accounts decide where money goes is about to matter a lot more. In a trial then subscription model, a $1.95 trial barely pays for its click. The money is in the renewal that is not refunded. If the bidding learns from trial starts, "Free" headlines will keep buying trials from people who expected a free tool.

I would start by sending the first renewal, net of refunds, back to Google and Bing as the conversion the bidding learns from. Then I would test a price-honest headline against "Free," judged on cost per renewed subscriber, and take the same scorecard to Taboola, Quora and your other products.
