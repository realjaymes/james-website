A memo for Dash0

Closing the Gap: Beyond Observability, on the Pages That Close the Sale

James Praise · Product Marketing Operator

Built entirely from Dash0's own public surfaces: dash0.com, the "Beyond Observability" founder post, the comparisons hub, docs, and changelog.

The memo

The pivot that hasn't landed

Mirko Novakovic's July 6 post, "Beyond Observability," states the repositioning plainly: "We're moving beyond observability. What is needed today is a closed loop from prompt to production." It's propagated to the homepage ("Ship more. Break less.") and the docs. It has not propagated to the 14 comparison pages where a buyer actively choosing between Dash0 and Datadog or Honeycomb lands and reads Dash0's own pitch for itself.

14comparison pages still running pre-pivot language
2independently-written self-descriptions checked, both anchored pre-July
0mentions of Agent0, Darkplane, or "Beyond Observability" on either

The Datadog page's self-description: "Dash0 is a modern observability platform built from the ground up on open standards... don't want to be handcuffed to a vendor's proprietary tech." The Honeycomb page, independently written: "a next-generation observability suite built from scratch with OpenTelemetry in mind... complete independence from vendor constraints." Same framing, same absence, two different pages.

"No proprietary lock-in" is a wedge any OpenTelemetry-native competitor can also claim. The AI SRE that fixes production before you notice is a wedge none of Dash0's listed alternatives currently own.

Templating the fix

Once the Datadog page carries this language, the same fix templates across the other 13 comparison pages without rebuilding each one from scratch. The same throughline should thread the changelog (currently a flat list, SignalControl and Agent0 Custom Skills sit ungrouped) and the pricing page's "Control the noise" tagline, which lists Agent0 credits as a line item but never connects them to the platform story.

Why I structured it this way

Bottom-of-funnel first

I picked the comparison pages over a homepage or blog fix because they're the highest-leverage real estate the pivot hasn't reached yet, closest to a buying decision, so the fix has a clear commercial rationale, not just a tone update. And I built it as a rewrite plus a short memo, not a new campaign, because the raw material (the founder's own words, Agent0's real capabilities, the Telegraph case study) already exists. The job here isn't inventing a new story, it's noticing the newest, truest story hasn't reached the page doing the actual selling, and fixing that with proof Dash0 already has.
