A plan for Homa

A Creator and Partner Plan Measured on Booked Tours

James Praise · Marketing and GTM Operator

I wrote this from Homa's public pages on 11 November 2026. I have no inside data and no affiliation with Homa. Every outside claim links to its source. Anything that needs Homa's own numbers is marked proof needed. The payment section describes a design constraint. It is not legal advice.

The read

What exists today

Homa already has the start of a creator program. Its referral page gives a buyer an extra $500 credit at closing when they come through someone's link, on top of the credit every Homa buyer gets. That is a ready-made reason for a creator's audience to use the link.

The refund is the storyThe homepage promises up to 2% back, with a 1% fee against a traditional 3%. Its example is a $15,000 credit on a $750,000 home.
There is nowhere for a creator to joinPaths like tryhoma.com/partners, /affiliates, /ambassadors and /creators returned 404 on 11 November 2026. No page tells a creator what to say or how they are paid.
Success is one numberThe job posting says marketing is measured on how many more home tours it drives. Paid media, SEO, social and design already have support.

The payment rules come first

Homa runs a brokerage and a mortgage lender, so referrals fall under the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA). Section 8 bans giving anything of value for referrals of settlement services. Paying fair market value for real work is allowed. In 2023 the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau fined a lender and a brokerage nearly $2M over marketing deals tied to referrals, as Mayer Brown reported.

Creators get a flat fee for the content they make. The fee never depends on tours, sign-ups or closings. The $500 goes to the buyer.

Before the first creator is paid, Homa's broker and counsel sign off on the terms, including state rules on rebates in California, Texas and Florida. Creators disclose the paid relationship, as the Federal Trade Commission's endorsement guides require.

The program

Who to recruit

Three creator types, 10 to 15 creators in the first round.

Creator typeWhereWhy their audience books tours
First-time buyer and personal finance creatorsTexas and FloridaSaving for a first home and worried about cost. Half of the people in a poll run by Arman Javaherian, Homa's CEO, picked lowering total cost as their top concern.
Tech and equity creatorsSan Francisco Bay AreaHigh prices mean a large refund. Arman wrote that most San Francisco homes sell over asking, so buyers are stretched.
New-build and relocation creatorsCaliforniaA new state bond under Prop 37 helps middle-income buyers of new builds, and those buyers still pay commission, as Arman pointed out.

What creators say

Each creator gets a short brief, not a script. They keep their own voice.

Brief 1: the refund in real numbers

A real buyer, a real price, the amount back. Arman has shared a buyer in Round Rock, Texas, who got $12,000 back on a $624,900 home. The homepage shows DJ in Tampa, a first-time buyer who saved $10,500. More stories with buyer consent proof needed.

Brief 2: how it works in one minute

The AI searches homes and works out the offer. A licensed agent signs off on every step. You book tours when it suits you.

Brief 3: the extra $500

Buy through my link and you get $500 more at closing, on top of Homa's normal credit.

Partners to test

Two partner tests in the first 30 days. Each partner is paid a flat fee for placement or services, under the same rules as creators.

  • A personal finance newsletter whose readers are saving for a home in Homa's states. It runs one sponsored section with a tracked link.
  • An employer or community group with members buying in Homa's states, such as a relocation or benefits program or an alumni group. It shares a guide to buying with Homa.

I've left partner names out on purpose. Names come after counsel approves the terms and after a first conversation.

Tracking to a booked tour

Every creator and partner gets their own link. Each step is tracked on its own, so a weak creator can be told apart from a weak page.

The 30-day test plan

  • Week 1. Map the tracking from link to booked tour. Get the payment terms cleared.
  • Week 2. Recruit 10 to 15 creators across the three types and send briefs.
  • Weeks 3 and 4. Content goes live, and the two partner tests start.
  • Day 30. Rank every creator and partner by booked home tours and cost per booked tour, compared with what a booked tour costs from paid social ads today proof needed. Keep the top third, cut the bottom third, and swap in new creators.

Why I built it this way

An outside plan, with the gaps marked

I don't know Homa's tours per week, where they come from today, or what a booked tour costs from paid social. So this plan sticks to what anyone can check and marks what needs inside data. It covers what I would do in the first month of the job.

I have built channels like this before. At Reform I built the affiliate program on PartnerStack from scratch, and a co-marketing partnership brought in 11% of new pipeline. At Cecure Intelligence, creator partnerships were part of a launch across five African countries. I've also been the creator a brand pays, running a sponsored showcase for Forage Mail in my newsletter.
