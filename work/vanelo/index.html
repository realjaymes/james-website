What the 60 Days Are

The research page shows what is in the 60 days and what comes next. See the scope →

The 60 days are an outbound pilot with two channels. The first is outbound to founders: email to the 5,000 founders PitchHub has already reached, cold email to founders who are raising, and daily replies on LinkedIn, X and Reddit. The second is outbound to partners: about 150 UK accelerators, angel networks and startup accountants who can promote Vanelo to every new group of founders they serve.

Outbound comes first for two reasons. It needs no ad spend, so the only cash is tools. It also builds the repeatable system you asked for: email sequences, follow-up emails that move a lead toward paying, messaging and outreach routines that keep running after day 60.

The Next Phase

The research page describes each of these in full. See the next phase →

Once the pilot shows results, a separate engagement adds three channels. Search engine optimization (SEO) builds pages and free tools that rank on Google for searches about raising investment, and it pays off over the following year. Founder referrals let a paying founder invite another, and both get one free month. Paid Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram send founders to your founders page.

Ads come last because your founders page needs fixes first. The video sits below the top section and has no button after it. On phones the header has no button and the main headline runs off the screen. The page also has no Meta pixel, the small piece of tracking code that tells Meta which visitors signed up. I would make eight changes before any ad runs, and Vanelo pays for the tools in this phase too.

The Numbers: 22, 67 or 150 New Founders

I project three cases for the pilot. Low uses the weakest rate at every step and gives 22 new paying founders by day 60. High uses the strongest and gives 150. Base, the most likely case, gives 67 and takes Vanelo from 17 to about 84. The outbound volumes come from the size of the market and not from a number I picked: about 6,000 UK companies close an equity round each year.

Your year-one target of 2,500 founders needs about 210 a month, and ads would have to bring most of them. Without ads the target is out of reach. With the next phase running, the base case for the first 60 days rises from 67 to 92.

What the Pilot Costs and How I Check It

The research page has the full budget, line by line. See the budget →

At base, the pilot costs about £430 in cash, all of it tools that Vanelo pays for. They are six email inboxes on three secondary domains, a sending tool, contact details for founders and email checking. A LinkedIn search tool and an email platform for the follow-up sequences are optional. There is no ad spend. Each new paying founder costs about £6 in cash. A paying founder is worth about £400 over their lifetime at base, and that value rests on my estimates until Vanelo has real data.

I read the numbers in week 4: replies, interested founders, sign-ups and cost for each channel. I move effort to the channels doing best and change the weakest message or list once. At day 60 we compare each channel's real cost per paying founder to this plan and decide which channels keep running and whether to start the next phase.
