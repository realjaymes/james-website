Positioning & Messaging

Full lifecycle rebuild: Arinka Travel's positioning and CRM overhaul

Arinka Travel is a travel agency and consultancy for individuals, families, students, and corporate travelers across the UK, Nigeria, and the African diaspora, helping them plan curated holiday experiences, secure visas, and navigate study-abroad journeys with confidence.

Service: Lifecycle Programs, Positioning & Messaging Stage: Seed Stage Industry: B2C Ecommerce Region: United Kingdom, Nigeria, Africa Visit website →
Context

Following rapid growth across holiday planning and study-abroad services, Arinka sought structured marketing systems, segmentation, automation, and lifecycle communication that aligned with its upgraded CRM workflows.

The challenge

Arinka Travel relied on manual communication, inconsistent lifecycle messaging, and an underutilized CRM workflow. The team needed clearer brand positioning, structured customer segmentation, automated campaigns, and integrated marketing systems for Holidays and Study Abroad services.

What I did

I refined Arinka's brand positioning, created structured messaging, built customer lifecycle systems, introduced segmentation for Holidays and Education, and implemented automation across Airtable and Brevo. I also designed 14-day nurture sequences, developed downloadable resources, and advised on social, content, and paid acquisition improvements with clear SOPs.

The results

Arinka received a complete positioning refresh, automated lifecycle flows, upgraded CRM segmentation, and a scalable email marketing engine for both key service lines, providing a foundation for consistent lead nurturing, improved customer experience, and higher conversion potential.

"James helped us refine our travel and study-abroad communication, automate key systems, and redesign our lifecycle structure. The clarity and execution improved how we engage customers and manage services across our CRM."

— Fisayo Bamidele, CEO, Arinka Travel
Summary impact
  • Complete brand positioning and messaging upgrade.
  • Segmented lifecycle automation for Holidays and Study Abroad.
  • CRM segmentation integrated with WPForms and Airtable.
  • A structured content and acquisition roadmap.
  • Downloadable resources to educate and nurture customers.
