The Pitch: Where I'd Start

MNTN hired its first Chief Revenue Officer this year to grow the small and mid-sized business segment. That changes the funnel under this role: more leads, smaller deals and faster cycles. Routing rules built for larger brand accounts tend to misfire quietly under that mix, and sender reputation slips as email volume climbs, usually before either shows up in pipeline numbers.

MNTN already has GTM engineering inside Business Systems, so I'd start by learning what that practice covers before proposing anything. Then I'd map the live Salesforce and LeanData rules against real lead volume, deal size and qualification signals from the new segment, and run a deliverability health check across authentication, sender reputation and domain warming. Any AI qualification step would sit on data that's already been checked, because automation built on unchecked data degrades without anyone noticing.
