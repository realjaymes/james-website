A brief for Coinrule

A Read on Coinrule's Affiliate Program and Content

James Praise · Marketing and GTM Operator

Live browser research on coinrule.com, September 2026.

What's already working

Coinrule's affiliate program runs at real scale.

7,952total affiliates, last 5 years
$327,212paid out since launch
30%commission, up to

Named affiliates with named numbers: one this year at $18,287, another having signed up 2,851 users. That's the hard part of a referral engine, already built and running.

What's actually missing

The blog is active, a post as recent as July 2026, but every single post is written by one person: the CEO, Gabriele Musella. That's not a content-strategy gap, it's a bandwidth ceiling.

A company with thousands of affiliates producing actual trading results has nobody turning those results into content.

What I'd build

Feed the blog from the affiliate program instead of running the two as separate systems. Coinrule already has named affiliates with named numbers, genuine raw material for content. A monthly piece built around one affiliate's actual results takes the CEO off the hook for every post and gives the affiliate program a second growth surface, content that a name-and-number story earns on its own, on top of the commission that already recruits them.

I checked the site directly instead of assuming what a crypto trading platform's growth gaps usually look like. The affiliate program turned out to be the strong part, already proving itself at scale. The actual bottleneck sits one layer over, in who has time to write about it.
