A search and AI answer audit for Taptap Send

Where Nigerian Senders Compare Apps

James Praise · Growth and Product Marketing, Lagos

On 5 October 2026 I ran 26 Google searches a Nigerian sender would type, from the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada, and screenshotted every results page. I also asked ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini five questions with web search on. Google data is desktop, English, top 20 results, with the AI Overview loaded. This uses no internal data.

The findings

The short version

Nigerian senders already look for Taptap Send by name. Brand search is larger than LemFi's in the UK (14,800 a month against 12,100) and in the US (22,200 against 8,100), from Google Ads keyword data checked the same day.

When they compare apps, the picture flips. Across 26 sender searches in three countries, taptapsend.com made Google's top 20 once. Google showed an AI Overview in 21 of the 26, and in the 16 that don't name Taptap in the question, it named Taptap twice.

1 of 26Nigerian sender searches where taptapsend.com ranked in Google's top 20 (UK, "best app to send money to nigeria," position 7)
2 of 16AI Overviews that named Taptap when the question didn't
About 55,000monthly UK and US searches for "pound to naira" and "dollar to naira," with no Taptap page to answer them

Where Taptap has a page that answers the question, Google cites it. Where it has none, the answer goes to whoever wrote one.

The plan

The page plan

  1. Sender-country Nigeria pages, UK, US and Canada first. A title that names the country, that country's limits and payment methods, the rate rendered in the page, a local FAQ, and the Trustpilot and Play Store proof.
  2. A naira rate hub for GBP, USD, CAD and EUR, showing what the recipient gets after Taptap's rate, with a plain explainer on rate versus fee. It answers the zero-fee doubt where senders already check the rate.
  3. Bank and wallet pages for the banks the Nigeria page names (Access, First Bank, Zenith), plus GTBank and the wallets Taptap pays out to.
  4. A Taptap vs LemFi page that makes Taptap's own case on rate, fees and coverage. Today the AI Overview writes that comparison from other people's pages. needs a compliance check
  5. A safety guide built from the security page and help articles: how transfer scams work, what Taptap never asks for, how holds work. It would rank beside the Reddit threads that rank today.
  6. Help articles opened up as guides, since help.taptapsend.com already ranks for comparison and trust searches.

Nigerian sender moments set the content calendar: school fees in September and January, December sends, and the days the naira moves sharply.

Next

How I'd measure it

The same 26 searches and five questions, re-run weekly: Taptap pages in the top 20, AI Overview mentions, AI Overview citations of taptapsend.com, and unprompted assistant mentions. Only Taptap can see the numbers that matter next to those: organic installs by sending country and rate-hub visits.

The first six weeks test the UK and US sender pages alone. If they move, the template rolls out to Canada, the EU, the UAE and Australia, then to bank names. If they don't, domain authority is the constraint, and the work shifts to earning coverage on the comparison sites that rank today.

Method and limits

Google results came from DataForSEO's live results endpoint with one location per country, and the screenshots from its screenshot endpoint for the same searches. Assistant answers came from its AI Optimization endpoints; Gemini's takes no location setting. This is one day's snapshot. AI Overviews change between runs, so the counts matter more than any single brand list. Three searches returned fewer than 20 results, and one came back partial. I haven't seen Taptap's Search Console, analytics or install data, so this is the outside view. The first thing I'd check is which queries already bring clicks to the Nigeria page.
