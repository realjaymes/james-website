Positioning & Messaging

1,900% MQL growth: Hank Robotics' GTM before commercialization

Hank Robotics is an AI-powered robotics company for property managers, facility operators, and hospitality businesses in Florida, automating high-risk manual tasks such as high-rise window and solar panel cleaning through drone and robotic systems.

Service: GTM & Product Launches, GTM Engineering & Automation, Growth Experiments, Lifecycle Programs, Positioning & Messaging, Website & Landing Pages Stage: Seed Stage Industry: Robotics Region: USA, North America Visit website →
Context

In 2025, Hank prepared to commercialize its first product line of drone-based cleaning systems, ahead of expansion into robotic servers for hotels. The company engaged me to build a go-to-market and SEO engine that would establish visibility, credibility, and qualified lead flow before product launch.

The challenge

Hank Robotics had a strong product roadmap and prototype, but no digital presence or inbound funnel. They needed to develop market positioning, build website credibility, and generate qualified B2B leads while preparing for commercialization.

What I did

As Fractional Product Marketing & Growth Consultant, I led a 90-day GTM and SEO execution program spanning SEO optimization, website enrichment, and lead generation. This included creating Hank's positioning framework, launching high-intent SEO campaigns, deploying 15 high-converting landing pages, building industry directory presence, and deploying Google and LinkedIn Ads for property managers and facility owners.

The results

Delivered measurable growth across every metric: 267.6% increase in organic clicks, 111.8% lift in impressions, 15% reduction in bounce rate, and 1,900% growth in MQLs, alongside a complete positioning and messaging framework for future campaigns.

"James gave our go-to-market strategy structure, clarity, and measurable traction. His SEO, positioning, lead gen, and automation frameworks helped Hank Robotics go from concept to visibility in just 90 days."

— Hanu Fejiro, CEO, Hank Robotics

  • 267 percent more clicks and 111 percent more impressions.
