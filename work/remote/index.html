The Pitch: Where I'd Start

Remote shipped AI Agents, a Custom Report Builder, Remote MCP and a command-line tool in a single quarter, and this role is asked to build GTM products with AI tools rather than only administer vendor software. An agent is only as trustworthy as the data it reads. A stack that spans Salesforce, HubSpot, LeanData, Outreach, ZoomInfo, Clay and Qualified was most likely added to one tool at a time, and Remote's whole promise to customers is compliance it owns end to end. Its own lead and account data should hold up to that promise.

I'd start with a data lineage map for the one AI surface that touches the most marketing data: what it reads, where that data comes from, and what governs its consent and retention today. Next comes an audit of the stack against real usage, finding what's duplicated, what's reconciled by hand and what breaks silently. Build-vs-buy calls come after that. I'd build the connecting layer only where it costs less to maintain than another tool, and the roadmap would come from what the audit finds.
