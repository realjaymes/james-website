Win the Hotels First

Jacaranda earns commission when a booking happens, and a guest has nothing to book until a hotel signs. I would start with three to five founding hotels in one city. Cape Town holds 21 of my 29 sample hotels, and the launch city is your call.

The First 90 Days and the Weekly Rhythm

In the first 30 days I would fix the name, agree the launch city, the commission and the hotel criteria with you, and contact the first hotels. By day 60 I would aim to sign three to five founding hotels (an estimate) and publish a page for each. By day 90 the second city starts, and the waitlist hears about the founding hotels.

Each week the systems would refresh the hotel list, repeat the AI check with its 25 questions and prepare a creative batch. A person would approve every message, visit the hotels and decide who joins, because Jacaranda says it visits before it lists. I would track hotels contacted, replies, visits and hotels signed, then waitlist registrations by city and source, and how often AI answers name Jacaranda.
