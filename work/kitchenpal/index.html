The Finding

Since January 2025, your team has replied to Play Store reviews with the same apology: "We're sorry that it wasn't clear that we have a paywall." One reply, dated 2025-01-02, adds that the paywall scan feature is "limited to only '100' scans per user." These replies were on the live listing as of 2026-10-10. Read the full plan →

The business logic in those replies is completely reasonable. They are also an admission that the limit was not communicated before people reached it. People found out mid-task that a feature they expected to work needed payment. The gap is sequencing, and it is fixable without touching a single feature.

The Fix Uses Copy You Already Wrote

Your Play Store description already lists the premium features explicitly. Most freemium apps skip that. The trouble is that almost nobody reads an app description end to end before installing. They install, hit the wall mid-task, and are not primed to read fine print. The information exists. It is not surfaced where someone needs it. See the two moves and the measurement plan →

Two moves: state the free limit before anyone reaches it, and replace the hard block with a paywall that remembers what the person was doing. Then a usage-triggered email lifecycle, because a permanent free tier has no deadline to build a sequence around.
