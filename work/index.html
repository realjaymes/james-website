01My background

Over the past 8 years, I've worked with early- to growth-stage companies across 3 continents (North America, Africa, and Europe)—leading go-to-market (GTM) strategy, customer acquisition, and demand generation efforts.

I've partnered with bootstrapped, seed, and Series A startups across B2B, SaaS, Fintech, HRTech, EdTech, HealthTech, AI, Robotics, MarTech, and ecommerce.

I've also been a founding marketer multiple times, building marketing functions from the ground up at Reform, FunnelEnvy, Bujeti (YC W23), Distrobird, and Cecure Intelligence, while mentoring founders through SBC Afritech and PAADC accelerators.

I'm a marketer with a combination of broad skills and deep channel expertise, something I like to visualize as a T, M, or Π shape. I wrote about this in my newsletter—a practical guide to finding and hiring marketers like myself, so you know what to look out for.

I've been trusted by 30+ founders, GTM operators and accelerators in North America, Europe & Africa.
