What Is Blocking Pipeline

I looked at Rayda's public channels. These findings stood out, and I would check each one with your team in week one.

Rayda's Google ads changed message during the year. In spring they said "Not Software, We Execute". By September they presented Rayda as device management software alongside Microsoft Intune and Jamf, in searches where a click costs $15 to $78.

The demo booking form asks nothing about company size or countries, so your team can't tell a 20-person startup from a 500-person company before the call.

Few people search Google for Rayda or its competitors by name. Waiting for buyers who are already looking won't produce enough pipeline on its own. See the evidence →

The First Tests and What They Cost

Phase one starts with two tests: a signal-led outbound system and a one-to-one podcast.

The outbound system pulls every warm lead into one list: people who engage with your posts, website visitors we can identify, guide downloaders, event and podcast guests, and search leads, plus a list of target companies I would build. I rank each lead on fit into three tiers. Tier 1 gets personal emails, LinkedIn messages, calls from your sales team and sometimes a gift. Tier 2 gets personalised email, LinkedIn and calls. Tier 3 gets an automated email sequence.

For the podcast, you or Dan interview one IT or operations lead from a target company per episode about a problem they solved.

The customer mixer follows in phase two, in London or San Francisco, once about 15 companies there are interested.

Phase one costs about $1,200 to $1,600. Phase two, up to about $8,400, is released only when tracking works and phase one hits agreed targets. The total stays under $10,000. See the budget →

The 90 Days

Weeks 1 to 3 set up measurement. That means tracking where each lead comes from and which tier it belongs to, fixing the demo form, building the list of target companies, warming up new email domains so outreach doesn't land in spam, and connecting post engagers and website visitors to one ranked list. Guest invitations for the podcast go out in week 2, and week 4 starts the signal-led outbound system and the first recordings. In weeks 6 to 8, we review the first results together. Paid search starts only after Rayda can track which ads lead to booked calls. The customer mixer in London or San Francisco runs in weeks 9 to 12 if enough companies there show interest, and gifts go out in January to Tier 1 companies already talking to Rayda.

Success means meetings your sales team judges worth pursuing, and the pipeline they create. Leads who show buying interest are the early signal. See how it runs →
