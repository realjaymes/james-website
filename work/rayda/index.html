What Is Blocking Pipeline

I looked at Rayda's public channels. These findings stood out, and I would check each one with your team in week one.

Rayda's Google ads changed message during the year. In spring they said "Not Software, We Execute". By September they presented Rayda as device management software alongside Microsoft Intune and Jamf, in searches where a click costs $15 to $78.

The demo booking form asks nothing about company size or countries, so your team can't tell a 20-person startup from a 500-person company before the call.

Few people search Google for Rayda or its competitors by name. Waiting for buyers who are already looking won't produce enough pipeline on its own. See the evidence →

The First Tests and What They Cost

Phase one starts with email outreach to 300 to 500 people at mid-sized companies that fit, and a one-to-one podcast. You or Dan would interview one IT or operations lead from a target company per episode about a problem they solved. I would also add company size and countries to the demo form. Your LinkedIn and X accounts would comment every day on posts by decision makers at the target companies, with a person reviewing each comment before it goes live, so they know your name before an email arrives.

We also discussed a customer mixer. You said it would be in London or San Francisco, where most of your current customers are, and timed for when you or Dan can be there or around the major events Rayda plans to attend next year. I would run it in phase two, after the email pilot and podcast guests show about 15 interested companies in the host city (my planning number), so Rayda invites companies it is already speaking with. I placed it there because you said Rayda's buyers live online, and the largest search demand is in the US. A version in Nigeria or elsewhere in Africa could follow later in 2027, once Rayda for Startups matures.

Phase one costs an estimated $1,000 to $1,500. Phase two, up to about $8,500, is released only when Rayda can track where each lead comes from and the phase one tests hit targets we agree in advance. The total stays under $10,000, not counting venue costs and staff time. See the budget →

The 90 Days

Weeks 1 to 3 set up measurement. That means tracking where each lead comes from, fixing the demo form, building the list of target companies, and warming up new email domains so outreach doesn't land in spam. Guest invitations for the podcast go out in week 2, and week 4 starts the email pilot and the first recordings. In weeks 6 to 8, we review the first results together. Paid search starts only after Rayda can track which ads lead to booked calls. The customer mixer in London or San Francisco runs in weeks 9 to 12 if enough companies there show interest, and gift boxes go out in January to companies already talking to Rayda.
