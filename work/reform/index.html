Positioning & Messaging

34% trial activation lift: Reform's post-acquisition SaaS repositioning

Reform is a conversion-focused form builder SaaS for non-technical growth marketers in B2B and B2C lead-generation organizations to deploy multi-step, interactive forms that maximize lead quality and quantity.

Service: GTM & Product Launches, GTM Engineering & Automation, Growth Experiments, Lifecycle Programs, Positioning & Messaging, Website & Landing Pages Stage: Bootstrapped Industry: MarTech / SaaS Region: USA, North America
Context

Reform was acquired by FunnelEnvy in 2023, and was repositioned and remodeled from an easy-to-use, no-code general-purpose form builder to focus on lead conversion use cases, improving form conversion rates while minimizing low-quality responses and spam.

The challenge

Following its acquisition by FunnelEnvy, Reform needed repositioning from a general-purpose form builder into a category-defining conversion-focused product, with a stronger PLG funnel, lifecycle automation, more disciplined GTM & feature launches, and expanded market presence through partnerships, SEO, and developer-focused positioning.

What I did

I redefined its positioning, developed new landing pages, built a PLG funnel with improved free trials and lifecycle automation, implemented new GTM & feature launch processes, led email migration from Userlist to Brevo, executed the legacy freemium plan sunset, ran acquisition experiments across SEO, integrations and partnerships, and expanded Reform's case study presence and partner program foundations.

The results

Delivered a 34% uplift in trial activation, 21% improvement in trial-to-paid conversion, migrated 72% of legacy freemium users to paid plans, published content at 100x previous velocity, drove a 48% increase in non-branded organic traffic, and positioned Reform competitively in CRO-focused SaaS.

"James played a critical role in Reform's repositioning and GTM execution, accelerating adoption while strengthening its role as a strategic growth driver for FunnelEnvy. His MIA newsletter was also the perfect platform to introduce Reform to non-technical growth marketers through founder interviews and in-depth MarTech product showcases."

— Arun Sivashankaran, CEO, Reform
Summary impact
  • 34 percent increase in trial activation and conversions.
  • 21 percent improvement in trial-to-paid conversion through Pro Plan Free Trial campaigns.
  • 72 percent of legacy freemium users migrated to paid plans through lifecycle automation.
  • 100x increase in content velocity and 48 percent rise in non-branded organic traffic.
  • 22 percent domain rating increase through AI-powered SEO and directory listings across 130+ platforms.
  • 29 percent boost in referral traffic through marketplace listings.
  • 19 percent more decision-stage leads through competitor comparison and integration SEO pages.
  • 26 percent increase in feature adoption and 18 percent improvement in onboarding activation.
