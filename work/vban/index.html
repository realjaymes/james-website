GTM & Product Launches

2,306 people reached: Vban's African fintech launch

Vban is a financial app for freelancers, creators, and remote professionals in Africa to simplify payments, manage client transactions, and streamline wallet funding. Built by Oval Finance / TM Payments Ltd, Vban launched in 2025 to address the growing demand for flexible, reliable financial tools in the gig economy.

Service: GTM & Product Launches Stage: Seed Stage Industry: FinTech / SaaS Region: Africa (HQ: Nigeria) Visit website →
Context

In 2025, Vban partnered with my MIA Product Showcase to amplify its launch, drive early adoption, and validate positioning within Africa's fintech ecosystem. The campaign combined media distribution (MIA Newsletter, Substack, LinkedIn) with creator amplification and community channels (WhatsApp, social media) to build brand awareness and product adoption.

The challenge

Vban needed to establish credibility at launch, drive brand awareness among freelancers and founders, and generate early adoption in a highly competitive African fintech space.

What I did

I executed a multi-channel Product Showcase campaign blending MIA's trusted newsletter distribution with creator-led storytelling and community amplification: an exclusive MIA Newsletter feature and Substack publication, a narrative-style LinkedIn newsletter and personal profile amplification, social carousels and posts across LinkedIn, X, and Instagram, and peer-to-peer validation via the MIA WhatsApp Community.

The results

The showcase delivered 2,306 newsletter recipients, 900+ feature views, 3,500+ social impressions, and 9+ verified clicks to the Vban app, with real community members trialing the product and sharing authentic feedback on onboarding and UX.

"Partnering with James and the MIA community gave Vban the visibility and credibility we needed at launch. The showcase didn't just build awareness, it sparked real conversations, hands-on adoption, and product feedback that validated our direction."

— Ayoni Jimoh, Product Marketing Manager, Oval Labs Inc.
