A concept for Leadfeeder

Leadfeeder Academy Certified

James Praise · Demand Generation & Community Operator

Built from Leadfeeder's own LearnUpon case study and a direct fetch of the current public Academy pages, leadfeeder.com/resources/academy.

The concept

A loop Leadfeeder already proved

Leadfeeder's own case study on their LMS vendor's site states it directly: "By leveraging Social Sharing in LearnUpon, customers are prompted to share certificates on LinkedIn or Twitter, increasing brand awareness and turning education into a demand generation channel... Leadfeeder Academy is contributing to new customer growth, with learners automatically awarded certifications upon successful course completion." That's not my framing, it's Leadfeeder's own credited claim, on a third party's site.

40+existing courses in the Academy catalog, no new content needed
0certificates, badges, or share prompts found on the current public pages
2rebrand cycles since (Leadfeeder → Dealfront → Leadfeeder) likely orphaning it

Why it's dark now

The Academy is live but filed under "Support" in the Resources mega-menu, described as "Platform best practices," not in primary nav, not framed as growth anywhere. Checked two live pages directly (the Academy landing page and the "Leadfeeder Basics" course page): no certificates, no completion badges, no LinkedIn/Twitter sharing prompts. The most plausible explanation is the 2022 Echobot merger and the two rebrand cycles since, exactly the kind of proven asset that goes quiet during years of brand and platform churn while nobody owns reviving it. Corroborating signal: the same "post-rebrand, nobody's reconnected the wires" pattern shows up elsewhere, the live `cognism-vs-dealfront` URL slug and a split G2 listing across two separate vendor pages.

The revival concept

PieceWhat it does
RepositioningMove the Academy from "Support" to a visible growth surface, primary nav or homepage placement
Certification loopRevive the certificate-on-completion mechanic against the existing 40+ course catalog
Share mechanicShareable LinkedIn certificate/badge format, UTM'd link back to the free trial
Activation tie-inTie course completion to the free Lite tier as an activation and expansion hook

Free-tier signup, in-product nudge to complete a short course, certificate unlock, one-click LinkedIn share, attributed new-trial signups. A demand-gen mechanic with a measurable loop, not just a training feature.

Why I structured it this way

I've run this exact loop

I didn't pick this concept because it sounded clever, I picked it because I've personally built and run the same mechanic in a different market. Marketing In Action compounds through peer referral and word of mouth, from zero to 5,000+ subscribers. That's the credibility bridge between "I noticed a gap" and "I know how to close it," which is what a PLG plus community-led demand gen hire needs to prove.
