GTM & Product Launches

13+ verified signups: Forage Mail's community-led product showcase

Forage Mail is an AI-powered inbox assistant for professionals, founders, and executives to declutter email, summarize newsletters, and automate inbox workflows, helping users save time, reduce overwhelm, and focus on what matters.

Service: GTM & Product Launches Stage: Seed Stage Industry: MarTech / SaaS Region: USA, North America Visit website →
Context

In 2025, Forage Mail engaged my MIA Product Showcase to validate adoption, build brand credibility, and generate trial conversions. The campaign combined media distribution (MIA Newsletter, Substack, LinkedIn) with creator amplification and community channels (WhatsApp, social media) to test market resonance and measure trial-to-paid intent.

The challenge

Forage Mail needed to stand out in a crowded AI productivity market by proving real user adoption, building credibility, and capturing early trial sign-ups among professionals, marketers, and business leaders.

What I did

I executed a multi-channel Product Showcase campaign that blended trusted media with creator-led storytelling: a long-form feature in the MIA Newsletter & Substack, a LinkedIn newsletter narrative ("How I Automated My Way Out of 8,000 Unread Emails"), a LinkedIn carousel and multi-platform social promo, and distribution via the MIA WhatsApp community for peer-to-peer amplification.

The results

The campaign delivered 1,689 newsletter recipients, 1,000+ feature views, 36 to 41% open rates (2x SaaS benchmarks), 3.4 to 4.3% CTR, and 13+ verified trial sign-ups, with community feedback confirming adoption and intent to upgrade after trials.

"James helped us validate Forage Mail with the right audience at the right time. The MIA showcase not only drove trial sign-ups but gave us authentic feedback from founders, marketers, and executives who genuinely needed our solution. His ability to combine media reach with community trust made the campaign a success and set us up for scale."

— Richie Bonilla, CEO, Forage Mail
