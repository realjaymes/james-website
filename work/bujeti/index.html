GTM & Product Launches

1,331 MQLs in 6 months: Bujeti's first real growth engine

Bujeti is a spend and expense management platform for finance teams at growing B2B companies in Africa to streamline expense tracking, budgeting, and approvals while improving financial visibility.

Service: GTM & Product Launches, Growth Experiments, Lifecycle Programs, Positioning & Messaging, Website & Landing Pages Stage: Seed Stage Industry: FinTech / SaaS Region: Africa (HQ: Nigeria)
Context

In late 2023, Bujeti prioritized building a growth engine to acquire new B2B customers, scale demand generation, and establish its category leadership through partnerships, events, and digital channels.

The challenge

Bujeti needed to scale customer acquisition rapidly in a competitive fintech landscape. The company lacked a repeatable inbound engine and relied heavily on outbound motions without structured growth marketing or lifecycle systems.

What I did

As Head of Growth & Marketing, I built and led the growth strategy, spanning acquisition, retention, and revenue optimization. I implemented product marketing, lifecycle marketing, CRO, SEO, events, and strategic partnerships, while aligning cross-functional teams to accelerate growth.

The results

Generated 1,331 MQLs in 6 months, increased new B2B customers by 500% in Q1 2024, improved landing page conversion by 20%, and reduced churn by 10%.

"James transformed our growth trajectory, bringing structure, velocity, and measurable impact across acquisition and retention. His ability to balance strategy with execution helped us scale quickly."

— Achille Arouko, CEO, Bujeti
Summary impact
  • 1,331 MQLs generated in 6 months.
  • 500 percent increase in new B2B customers in Q1 2024.
  • 20 percent uplift in landing page conversion and 18 percent improvement in product conversion.
  • 10 percent reduction in churn and 20 percent increase in engagement.
  • Scalable inbound, lifecycle, and partnership systems supporting ongoing category leadership.
