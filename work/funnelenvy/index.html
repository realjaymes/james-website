GTM & Product Launches

40% more qualified pipeline: FunnelEnvy's growth, GTM, and demand gen

FunnelEnvy is a Revenue Funnel Optimization agency for demand generation marketers at B2B SaaS and technology companies in the US and Europe, helping them accelerate pipeline velocity and revenue growth.

Service: GTM & Product Launches, GTM Engineering & Automation, Growth Experiments, Lifecycle Programs, Positioning & Messaging, Website & Landing Pages Stage: Bootstrapped Industry: B2B Tech Services Region: USA, North America & Europe
Context

FunnelEnvy acquired Reform in 2023 to integrate Reform's lead capture form capabilities into its broader Revenue Funnel Optimization services. In 2026, FunnelEnvy launched GrowthNode.ai as a new brand under the Celerius Group, positioning it as an AI growth partner for teams building and deploying agentic workflows.

The challenge

FunnelEnvy needed someone to manage Growth, GTM, and Demand Gen to generate qualified leads for their services. They also needed a Product Marketing Manager to oversee Reform's PLG and product marketing initiatives post-acquisition. As the engagement evolved, the scope expanded to a full rebrand, launching a new AI-focused brand (GrowthNode.ai), and building agentic growth infrastructure across the Celerius Group.

What I did

I implemented structured demand gen campaigns, an experimentation framework, and multi-brand measurement systems, while managing Reform's PLG funnel, repositioning, lifecycle automation, and GTM & product launches to align both brands strategically. I led the FunnelEnvy rebrand, launched GrowthNode.ai from zero, built a centralized brand knowledge repo, and developed agentic workflows for content distribution and growth operations.

The results

Delivered a steady pipeline of qualified enterprise leads (+40% QoQ), stronger brand positioning, measurable funnel visibility, 25% improvement in Reform's trial-to-paid conversion, a complete brand refresh, and a new AI growth brand launched and deployed.

"James brought clarity and execution to our growth and product marketing efforts, helping FunnelEnvy and Reform scale pipeline impact post-acquisition. His MIA newsletter was also the perfect platform to introduce Reform to non-technical growth marketers and gather valuable product feedback."

— Arun Sivashankaran, CEO, FunnelEnvy
