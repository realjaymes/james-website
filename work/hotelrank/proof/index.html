Overview: HotelRank.ai / Lighthouse Connect AI

HotelRank.ai tracks how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok rank, recommend, and describe a hotel, benchmarks that against nearby competitors, and shows whether AI-driven interest converts to a direct booking or leaks to an online travel agency. Three tiers: Essential (€58/month annual, 1-3 hotels, ChatGPT and Gemini only), Pro (€166/month annual, up to 50 hotels, all platforms, most popular), and Enterprise (custom, white-label, single sign-on). On May 28, 2026, Lighthouse acquired HotelRank.ai to fold its visibility metrics into Connect AI, Lighthouse's product for getting hotels discovered and bookable through AI travel platforms, as the measurement layer underneath it. Lighthouse CEO Sean Fitzpatrick framed it directly: "Connect AI gives hotels the ability to be discovered and booked through AI travel platforms. Hotelrank.ai completes the picture by showing them exactly how they're performing in this new channel."

In one line: the only AI-visibility platform purpose-built for hospitality's actual buying signals, booking-path attribution and OTA leakage, now backed by Lighthouse's distribution.

Why this matters to the buyer: a hotel GM cares which channel an AI-driven booking actually lands on, not brand-mention volume in the abstract. And Otterly is genuinely cheaper to try, a real objection a rep needs an answer for.

Why we win

  1. Booking-path attribution is the actual business question, and no general-purpose tool answers it. A hotel doesn't want to know it was mentioned, it wants to know whether that mention became a direct booking or leaked to an OTA and its commission. Proof needed: a real before/after booking-recovery number from a signed hotel.
  2. The Lighthouse acquisition just handed the product 80,000+ hotels of distribution that Profound, Otterly, and Scrunch have no equivalent vertical channel into. Proof needed: an actual signed reference hotel sourced through Lighthouse's own base.
  3. Traveler personas map directly to how a revenue manager already segments demand (business vs. leisure, length of stay, booking window). None of the three general-purpose tools reviewed offer a hospitality-specific persona layer.

Customer pain points

  • No visibility into how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, or Grok describe or rank the property, and no way to catch a factually wrong AI answer about amenities or location.
  • Fear of AI-driven bookings leaking to an OTA's commission instead of landing direct, with no current way to measure how often that happens.
  • A hotel group already paying for a horizontal AI-visibility tool and needing a reason the hospitality-specific version is worth adding or switching to.

Handling objections

"We already use Otterly, Profound, or Scrunch for AI visibility across our whole brand."
None of the three track booking-path attribution or OTA leakage, the two numbers that actually move a hotel's revenue. A horizontal tool can say the hotel got mentioned; it can't say whether that mention converted to a direct booking or handed the OTA a commission. Proof needed: a side-by-side pull of the same hotel's HotelRank.ai report against an Otterly or Profound report on the same query set.

"Otterly starts at $29/month, why would we pay €58 to €166?"
Otterly's Lite tier tracks 4 engines with no booking-path or OTA-leakage data. The real objection is price-to-relevance, not price alone. Proof needed: a specific dollars-of-OTA-commission-recovered figure once a signed customer has one.

"We're a Lighthouse Connect AI customer already, is this a separate purchase?"
This is a real, current gap in the public-facing messaging: as of this research, neither HotelRank.ai's own site nor Lighthouse's Connect AI page visibly explains how the two products and their pricing coexist for a buyer evaluating both. Proof needed: the actual internal packaging answer.

Questions to ask

  1. Are you already tracking AI visibility with another tool, and if so, does it tell you whether an AI-driven booking landed direct or went to an OTA?
  2. Is this evaluation happening at the property level or the group/chain level?
  3. Has a specific AI answer about your property ever been factually wrong, in a way you've noticed or a guest has mentioned?

Segment note

This card applies to the mid-market and hotel-group segment (Pro tier and above), where a marketing or revenue-management team is most likely already evaluating a horizontal AI-visibility tool. The Essential-tier independent-hotel buyer is less likely to be comparing against Profound or Scrunch, both enterprise-leaning on price, and more likely comparing against doing nothing at all, or against Otterly's Lite tier specifically.
