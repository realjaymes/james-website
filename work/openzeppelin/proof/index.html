A proof map for OpenZeppelin

Six buyer pages, one set of proof

James Praise · Product marketing, go-to-market and AI systems

I built this from openzeppelin.com only, read on 10 November 2026. I have seen none of your internal proof, so the items marked "proof needed" are where your own data would go.

What I found

OpenZeppelin now has a page for each institutional buyer: banks, asset managers, payment networks, tokenization platforms, capital markets infrastructure, and fintechs and neobanks. Each page speaks to its buyer's risks in that buyer's language. The proof underneath is the same everywhere.

All six pages carry the same two quotes, from Jason Guthrie at WisdomTree and Zach Short at DTCC. None links to a customer story. Of the ten customer stories on the site, two are financial institutions, Crédit Agricole CACEIS and WisdomTree. The strongest institutional story, CACEIS Bank's euro stablecoin, does not appear on the banks page.

One number also changes inside each page. The body copy says 10 of the top 10 tokenized money market funds build on OpenZeppelin Contracts, and the stat block on the same page says 9 of the top 10. The home page uses 9. A risk reader who sees a figure change on one page starts checking the rest.

How it stays current

A map like this goes stale the week a new story publishes. The workflow I would build keeps it current.

First, when a customer story, launch, partnership or research post goes live, an agent reads it and tags the segments it proves.

Second, it drafts the change: a new proof line for each tagged segment page and an updated proof block for each segment's one-pager.

Third, it checks every figure against one source list, so a number that appears two ways gets flagged before it ships.

Fourth, a marketer approves each change before it reaches the website or a sales kit. Nothing publishes without a person signing off.

The Chainlink partnership (20 October) and confidential balances on Stellar (22 October), both on your news page, would have been the first two items through it.
