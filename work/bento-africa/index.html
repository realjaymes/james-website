Lifecycle Programs

194% peak MoM growth: Bento's full-funnel AARRR rebuild

Bento is a payroll and human resources management platform for businesses in Africa, automating payroll, managing HR operations, and improving workforce efficiency across Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and Rwanda.

Service: GTM & Product Launches, Growth Experiments, Lifecycle Programs, Positioning & Messaging, Website & Landing Pages Stage: Seed Stage Industry: HR Tech / FinTech / SaaS Region: Africa (HQ: Nigeria) Visit website →
Context

In 2022, Bento focused on scaling adoption and retention in multiple African markets while improving funnel performance and preparing for rapid regional growth.

The challenge

Bento needed to optimize its acquisition and retention funnel (the AARRR model) to scale adoption across African markets. Despite growth, conversion and retention bottlenecks limited pipeline velocity and customer lifetime value.

What I did

As Growth & Product Marketing Manager, I implemented data-driven strategies across acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, and referral. This included designing and launching a lead scoring system, building lifecycle nurturing programs, optimizing product engagement through microcopy, and running growth experiments across multiple markets.

The results

Achieved a 50% increase in SQLs, 25% increase in sales conversions, improved retention metrics (DAU/MAU, DAU/WAU, WAU/MAU), and delivered peak user growth of 194.56% MoM with a compound growth rate of 330.94%.

"James helped us unlock scalable growth by combining product marketing discipline with data-driven experimentation. His work on lead nurturing and retention made a tangible impact on our funnel performance."

— Ebun Okubanjo, CEO, Bento
Summary impact
  • 50 percent increase in SQL volume.
  • 25 percent improvement in sales conversions.
  • Stronger retention across DAU, WAU, and MAU ratios.
  • Peak monthly user growth of 194.56 percent with a compound growth rate of 330.94 percent.
  • Scalable growth playbooks for expansion across Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and Rwanda.
