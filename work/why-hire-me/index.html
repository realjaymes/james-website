The Pattern

Over the past 8 years, I've worked with young and growing companies across 3 continents (North America, Africa, and Europe). Most brought me in during a period of change. They needed a reliable way to grow and had to build it from the ground up.

I've been a company's first marketer several times. I built marketing from the ground up at Reform, FunnelEnvy, Bujeti (YC W23), Distrobird, and Cecure Intelligence. I have also mentored founders through the SBC Afritech and PAADC programs. Founders and hiring managers bring me in when there is no plan yet, and I build one that fits their business.

How I Work

I start every job by finding out what is stopping the company from growing. The problem could be how the product is explained, where we are trying to reach people, the product itself, or the team. I work that out before I start a campaign.

I know a little about many parts of marketing and a lot about a few of them. I picture that range as a T, M, or Π shape, which I explained in my newsletter. I can plan how a product reaches the market, explain why people should buy it, keep in touch with customers, and run paid ads. I also know those parts well enough to do the work myself.

I build systems that keep working. I communicate clearly and make decisions with the long term in mind. That is the standard I use for all my work.
