A quick read for AIVideo.com

A Read of AIVideo.com's Own Meta Ads

James Praise · Marketing and GTM Operator

Live browser research via Meta Ad Library, exact-phrase "AIVideo.com" advertiser search, confirmed by the consistent logo and destination link across every result (a broad keyword search returned ~43,000 unrelated ads before narrowing).

What's running

14 active ads, all funneling to a "Book a call" link. The video treatments actually vary, a talking-head testimonial, a text-callout style ("JAKE! You still haven't paid us."), a product-demo cut ("First, we made winning animated ads"). But almost every one opens on one of two headlines: "I spent $800k on AI ads !!" or "higher quality AI ads?"

The observation

A company that sells AI-made video ads to other businesses is testing video treatments on its own account but not testing the hook that has to earn the click before any of that video plays.

What I'd test

Pair each existing video treatment against 2-3 new opening lines instead of running all of them under the same one or two hooks. Cheap to set up, since the video assets already exist, and it directly answers the question a prospective client would ask a video-ad company handling their account: do you test your own hooks as hard as you test your visuals.
