The Pitch: Win the Answer Where Ads Can't Run

Paxful shut down in November 2025, and its users in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and Cameroon are still deciding where to trade. Many of them ask Google or an AI assistant first. Crypto ads are restricted in most of these markets, so the organic answer ends up doing the ad's job. I ran 12 of those searches for this pitch. Read the teardown →

The pattern holds across markets. Where Binance has a current, plainly written local page, the answer uses it. Binance's own post answers whether it's legal in South Africa, and a Binance P2P page ranks first in Ghana for buying USDT with MTN Mobile Money. Where that page doesn't exist, someone else writes the answer. In Nigeria it's Breet, a cash-out app. In French and Swahili it's local mobile-money players and YouTube.

In my first 90 days I'd track a fixed set of searches every week by market and language, and get wrong third-party claims corrected. Then I'd publish payment-rail pages in Swahili and French, and in Hausa once compliance signs off. The searches that convert would feed local paid keywords and creative, measured on first trades by country.
