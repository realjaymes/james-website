A plan for MinIO

EMEA field and partner plan, GITEX to mid-2027

James Praise · Growth, go-to-market and AI systems

I built this from your public pages, checked on 14 November 2026. I have not seen your pipeline, budget or partner data, so the partner and event choices below are a starting point to test against those numbers in the first month.

You are hiring someone to turn global launches and alliances into EMEA pipeline, mostly through events and partners. This page is how I would start. It shows where your partners sit today and which events to build around. It then sets out three plays to run with your alliances and one way to measure all of it.

How the loop works

Each quarter follows the same loop. Plan from the deals sales is working, run three kinds of event, turn attendance into meetings and registered deals, then let cost per opportunity decide the next quarter. Each box links to the section that explains it.

1 PLAN

  1. 1 Pull EMEA pipelineOpen deals by segment and stage, with sales
  2. 2 Tier the partnersBy registered deals and account coverage
  3. 3 Pick the playsSovereign AI, Databricks, edition migration

2 RUN EACH QUARTER

  1. A Flagship showGITEX, LEAP, ISC or a partner summit
  2. B Partner side eventDinner or roundtable co-hosted by a partner
  3. C Technical workshopHands-on session for engineers

3 CONVERT

  1. D Book meetingsFollow-up within five working days
  2. E Register the dealPartner registers it under the joint plan
  3. F Opportunity created?Yes: carry on. No: Back to nurture with the partner.

4 MEASURE

  1. 4 Read the dashboardSourced and influenced pipeline by program
  2. 5 Cost per opportunityCompare each event with the others

First test: a GITEX dinner

GITEX is the next EMEA date on your calendar. I would add a 15 to 20 seat dinner co-hosted with one Gulf partner, on keeping AI data in-country. The guest list comes from accounts sales and the partner agree on before invitations go out.

Every guest gets a meeting request within five working days. I would count booked meetings and opportunities, then compare cost per opportunity with the booth. That one comparison tells you how much of next year's budget should go to side events.

Three plays to run with your alliances

Sovereign AI. You announced the HUMAIN partnership at LEAP 2026, around HUMAIN's sovereign data platform. In Europe, the EU AI gigafactories call closed on 12 November, and the selected projects will be building sovereign AI capacity. I would run a briefing series with NVIDIA and HUMAIN for public sector and regulated buyers in both regions.

On-premises data for Databricks. Databricks named you its 2026 ISV Storage Partner of the Year. Your campaign page already shows Databricks reading on-premises tables through AIStor Table Sharing. I would run that story as roundtables with Databricks' regional team, for banks and manufacturers who cannot move data to the cloud.

Moving off the community edition. The free edition entered maintenance mode in late 2025. EMEA teams still running it have to choose what comes next. I would run partner-led migration workshops for them, so the partner books the follow-up and registers the deal.

First 90 days

First, I would agree the EMEA pipeline target with sales and pull the open deals by segment and stage. Second, I would tier the partners by deals registered in the last four quarters. Third, I would run the GITEX dinner and read its numbers against the booth.

By day 90, the top partners would have signed joint plans, the next two quarters would be set on the loop above, and the dashboard would be live with sales.
