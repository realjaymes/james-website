A concept for ElevenLabs

Shareable Voice Moment: A Growth Loop for ElevenReader

James Praise · Growth & Partnerships Operator

Built entirely from ElevenReader's own public surfaces: elevenreader.io, its pricing and referral pages and the App Store listing, rechecked on 10 October 2026.

The concept

The moment with no button

ElevenReader's App Store listing leads with celebrity narrators: Michael Caine, James Dean, Maya Angelou. That's the product's single most distinctive, hardest-to-copy asset, licensed voices that are hard for a competitor to replicate. On 10 October 2026 I checked the homepage and pricing page for a share, clip, or send-to-a-friend mechanic tied to that moment and found none. The App Store release notes do mention a share control in playback, share-a-collection links and earning hours through referrals, so the app has some sharing. This concept is about attaching the offer to the celebrity-voice moment itself. I could not test that from outside the app.

25%referral discount on the Ultra plan, stated in a single FAQ answer on the pricing page
Editors' ChoiceApp Store badge the listing carries, above a celebrity-voice lead

The referral program that does exist rewards the subscription relationship, not the content moment: "If you refer a friend and they join through your link, both of you will earn 25% off your Ultra plan." (pricing page) The offer also has its own referral page, and the App Store description does not mention it. Two real assets, a licensed celebrity voice and a working discount, sit one product decision apart, and I found nothing on the public pages that connects them.

The trigger-to-share flow

The fix isn't a new referral program, ElevenReader already has one. It's designing the specific moment where sharing becomes the obvious next action:

TriggerShareable objectAttached incentive
End of a celebrity-narrated sampleAuto-generated short clip + a visual card carrying voice name, quote, and a deep linkThe existing 25% referral offer, surfaced at the moment of delight instead of sitting in one FAQ answer

This is buildable as a real concept deliverable without ElevenReader's internal app or backend: the flow, the card design, and the deep-link/referral wiring can be specified precisely enough for a product or growth engineer to build against.

Sizing the opportunity

ElevenReader's App Store listing says its celebrity voices come "through partnerships with the celebrities and their estates," so the asset is already licensed and already the lead feature of the listing. This concept doesn't ask for new investment, it asks for the acquisition mechanism that asset was always missing.

Why I structured it this way

A loop, not a new program

I didn't pitch "you should have a referral program," ElevenReader already has one. The specific finding is that, on the public pages I checked, the referral mechanic and the product's most distinctive asset are not connected, and the offer is not placed at the moment of delight. That's a sharper, more buildable brief than a generic growth-loop deck, and it's the same discipline I bring to every partnership: treat it as a hypothesis that needs proving, and build the measurement and the trigger into the mechanic before calling it a channel.
