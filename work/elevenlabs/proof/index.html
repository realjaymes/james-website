A concept for ElevenLabs

Shareable Voice Moment: A Growth Loop for ElevenReader

James Praise · Growth & Partnerships Operator

Built entirely from ElevenReader's own public surfaces: elevenreader.io, its pricing and referral pages, the App Store and Google Play listings, and the GenFM blog post.

The concept

The moment with no button

ElevenReader's App Store listing leads with celebrity narrators: Michael Caine, James Dean, Maya Angelou. That's the product's single most distinctive, hardest-to-copy asset, licensed voices almost no competitor can legally replicate. I checked the homepage, both app store listings, and the GenFM podcast-generation blog post for any share, clip, or send-to-a-friend mechanic tied to that moment. There isn't one.

4.7 / 10K+App Store rating and review count, Editors' Choice
25%referral discount that exists, buried in one sentence on the pricing page
0share prompts found anywhere tied to the celebrity-voice moment itself

The referral program that does exist rewards the subscription relationship, not the content moment: "If you refer a friend and they join through your link, both of you will earn 25% off your Ultra plan." It's not on the homepage, not in either app store listing, and its own dedicated landing page isn't linked from anywhere else checked. Two real assets, a licensed celebrity voice and a working discount, sitting one product decision apart from each other and never connected.

The trigger-to-share flow

The fix isn't a new referral program, ElevenReader already has one. It's designing the specific moment where sharing becomes the obvious next action:

TriggerShareable objectAttached incentive
End of a celebrity-narrated sample, or a completed GenFM episodeAuto-generated short clip + a visual card carrying voice name, quote, and a deep linkThe existing 25% referral offer, surfaced at the moment of delight instead of buried on the pricing page

This is buildable as a real concept deliverable without ElevenReader's internal app or backend: the flow, the card design, and the deep-link/referral wiring can be specified precisely enough for a product or growth engineer to build against.

Sizing the opportunity

ElevenReader is already ElevenLabs' fastest-growing app, per the company's own language, with real distribution precedent (telecom and bank bundling) proving partnership-led growth already works here. The celebrity-voice asset is already paid for and already marketed as the headline feature. This concept doesn't ask for new investment, it asks for the acquisition mechanism that asset was always missing.

Why I structured it this way

A loop, not a new program

I didn't pitch "you should have a referral program," ElevenReader already has one. The specific, evidence-backed finding is that the referral mechanic and the product's single most distinctive asset have never been connected, and neither is placed anywhere a user would actually encounter it at the moment of delight. That's a sharper, more buildable brief than a generic growth-loop deck, and it's the same discipline I bring to every partnership: treat it as a hypothesis that needs proving, and build the measurement and the trigger into the mechanic before calling it a channel.
