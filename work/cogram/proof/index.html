A brief for Cogram

A Read of Cogram's Content and Case Studies

James Praise · Marketing and GTM Operator

Live browser research on cogram.com, September 2026, cross-checked against the company's known integrations (Procore, Deltek, Autodesk) and named customers.

What's already working

Cogram's case-studies page has four named, quoted customers: CDH Partners, JacobsWyper Architects, Energyficient Systems, KIRKOR Architects and Planners. Named people, direct quotes about accuracy and repeatability. That's the hard part of B2B proof, already done.

What's stalled

Case studies

4 named customers, direct quotes, current

Blog

One post in the eleven months since October 2025, and most earlier posts are release notes

A customer-proof engine is running. The content engine that was supposed to carry it further has nearly stopped.

Where I'd point the first 60 days

The case studies already answer the question a content strategy usually has to invent from nothing: which firms trust Cogram, and why, in their own words. I'd turn each existing case study into two or three smaller pieces before touching a new content calendar. A newsletter issue built around one firm's actual workflow. A short piece on the specific administrative task that case study solved. The raw material already exists, sitting in four case studies and a blog that has published once since October 2025.

I checked the blog and the case-studies page separately instead of assuming one dormant page meant the whole content motion was empty. The customer-proof side is current and well-populated. The narrower finding, that proof exists but publishing has slowed to a trickle, points directly at what the first 60 days should be: get the existing pipeline moving again before spending time building a second one.
