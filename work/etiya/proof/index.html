A North American Reference Ladder

James Praise · Marketing and GTM Operator

This draft uses public sources only. I have not seen Etiya's pipeline, win and loss data or customer interviews, so anything that needs them is marked [proof needed]. Figures that only Etiya publishes are labelled as Etiya's own.

The draft

The question a buyer asks

The North American brands Etiya shows first are Videotron, Fizz and Freedom Mobile. All three belong to Quebecor. Quebecor raised its stake in Etiya to 70% on 21 April 2026.

A buyer at another operator can read that as deep use inside one group, or as a company running its parent's systems. The ladder below puts each public reference in the order an outside buyer can check it.

The opening paragraph

This is the paragraph I would put at the top of a sales conversation with an independent North American operator.

Fizz went from concept to launch in 10 months on Etiya's platform, and Videotron also used it for Helix. Those are Quebecor brands, so the fair question is whether the platform works for an operator that does not own Etiya.

Bouygues Telecom is an independent French operator with 22.9 million subscribers, and it announced in 2022 that three of its brands would share one Etiya platform. [proof needed: Bouygues go-live date and result.]

Quebecor raised its stake in Etiya to 70% in April 2026 to support one platform across Videotron, Freedom and Fizz.

What I would need from Etiya

None of this is public, and each item would change the ladder.

  • The Bouygues Telecom go-live date and one measured result. [proof needed]
  • Whether any North American operator outside Quebecor is live or in contract. [proof needed]
  • Which reference North American buyers ask for first, from sales conversations. [proof needed]
  • The Fizz and Helix figures with a named source behind each. [proof needed]
  • Which Freedom Mobile milestones Etiya can talk about. [proof needed]

Why I built it this way

Public sources only

Every figure on this page links to where it was published, and I opened each source before linking it. Where Etiya is the only publisher, the page says so.

The ladder is a hypothesis. Interviews with Etiya's sales team would show which reference North American buyers ask for first, and whether the order changes.
