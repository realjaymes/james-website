A working brief for Founder Institute

Founder Story to Applicant Funnel

James Praise · Marketing, community and content systems

This brief uses public material from Founder Institute's job page, FI 50, graduate directory, events and video channel. Internal conversion rates and workflow details remain unknown, so the system starts with a small pilot and measures what happens.

The raw material already exists

Your job page names the opportunity clearly: thousands of founder stories remain unheard, while the podcast and global events already produce more material. The 2026 FI 50 adds current milestones from founders across 32 countries. The graduate directory holds the broader archive.

So I would start by giving every useful story one way into a single editorial system, before producing anything new.

One editorial intake

Graduate news, chapter submissions, event quotes and podcast timestamps enter one queue. Each record holds the founder, company, country, founder stage, milestone, source link, proof status, permission status and the next application or event window.

FieldWhy it exists
Founder stageMatches the lesson and call to action to the reader's current problem.
Proof and permissionKeeps a good story accurate and safe to publish.

One weekly theme

One theme connects the strongest story to an immediate founder problem. "Building from industry knowledge" could carry a graduate milestone, a podcast clip, a live event and a practical newsletter section. The theme makes the week coherent without copying the same caption into every channel.

A job for each channel

EventsLet a prospective founder ask questions and meet the network live.

The video archive and events calendar give this system a continuing source of expert moments and live calls to action.

The conversion path

Every asset keeps its source, story, theme, channel and campaign identifier. The weekly report then follows event registrations, newsletter signups, started applications and completed applications. Impressions remain useful context, but the deciding question is which stories move qualified founders to the next step.

The 24-hour welcome

An accepted founder receives a personal message within 24 hours, as the role requires. The message names something specific from the application and points to one useful event or resource. The marketing team also follows the founder's public work and engages when there is something real to add.

For a new founder, that message is the first sign that the network notices them. Those conversations also turn up graduate news and the founders most likely to refer others.

The first pilot

Start with one FI 50 founder, one upcoming event and one podcast or video moment. Produce the LinkedIn story, Instagram package, newsletter section and site story from the same verified record. Give every asset one campaign identifier and one next action.

Run the pilot for two weekly themes. Use the first results to decide which story sources and calls to action deserve more production time.
