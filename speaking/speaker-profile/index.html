About James

James Praise is a seasoned marketing strategist and mentor who has dedicated his career to fostering the growth and success of businesses and individuals across Africa, and aims to leverage his expertise in strategic communication and community-building to contribute to the mission of spreading ideas worth sharing (just like TEDx's mission).

Driven by a passion for fostering innovation and entrepreneurship in Africa, James has been privileged to serve as a mentor to entrepreneurs and operators, engaging with various communities such as Startupbootcamp Afritech (SBC), Professor Ayodele Awojobi Design Competition (PAADC), Black Ops, Tech Sales & Partnerships Community (TSPC), Black Builders Network, ForbesBLK, RevGenius, Association of Digital Marketing Professionals (ADMARP), RevGrowth Network, and GrowthHackers.Africa.

James has built communities by sharing everything he knows about marketing, productivity, and personal growth—templates, AI-assisted tools, mental models, frameworks, and more. He publishes the insights in his newsletters—Marketing In Action, and RealTalk.

James' commitment to empowering others is reflected in his numerous speaking engagements, panels, and mentorship roles, a few of which are highlighted here.

James' contributions have also been recognized through media features in esteemed publications such as Africa Fintech Summit, BusinessDay, Vanguard News, and others, where he has provided valuable insights on pressing topics. See here.

James is Passionate About These Topics

1. How to Build a Successful Career in Nigeria (Despite the System)

  • Nigeria's education system is outdated, and guidance is rare. Most young people are thrown into career paths by JAMB and circumstance.
  • With 158 million youth and 53% under/unemployed, we need a better path forward.
  • From Biochemistry to Marketing: my unconventional journey and the lessons I learned.
  • How to break free from the "Nigerian default" and chart your own career—whether as a tech professional, a digital creator, an entrepreneur, or a corporate professional.
  • Practical frameworks and mindset shifts for finding direction and building leverage.
  • Why youth empowerment requires systems, not just skills.

2. Systems Over Hustle: A New Way to Think About Growth, Productivity & Clarity

  • We're not lacking ideas—we're drowning in them. What we need are systems we can trust.
  • Why I started documenting strategies, templates, frameworks, and mental models, and how that turned into a movement.
  • How Marketing In Action and RealTalk became digital ecosystems for clarity and action.
  • Decision-making as a skill: how to move fast without rushing.
  • Frameworks like Life OS, Decision Trees, and Energy Audits that anyone can use to gain clarity.

3. Think Like a Startup: How to Build a Life Operating System

  • What if you ran your life like a startup?
  • The systems I use to track energy, goals, and progress: from OKRs to Google Docs & Notion dashboards.
  • Life Design > Life Planning: a framework for thinking long-term while acting in sprints.
  • Why every young African should think like a builder—even outside of tech.

4. Content as a Product: A Framework for Building Communities that Thrive

  • Content isn't just a marketing tactic—it's the product.
  • How I built Marketing In Action by treating content like a startup: structured, iterative, and community-led.
  • The 30% Juice Rule: Your best content goes beyond your product—it builds trust and solves real problems.
  • From audience to advocates: A playbook for turning insight into impact.
  • Content in the age of AI: Why connection beats distribution.

5. AI Isn't Coming—It's Here: Thriving in the Age of Augmented Creativity

  • AI will change the way we create—but it won't replace human insight.
  • How to pair AI tools with human frameworks to supercharge creativity and decision-making.
  • How I use AI in content, strategy, and product workflows—and how others can do the same.
  • Insight vs Information: How to stand out when anyone can generate content.

6. Building Africa's Growth Engine: Empowering the Next Generation through Knowledge

  • Africa doesn't just need more startups—it needs more mentors, more knowledge access, more structured ecosystems.
  • Insights from mentoring across platforms like ADPlist, Growth Skool, SBC Afritech, and more.
  • The power of community-led growth and knowledge democratization.
  • How to build a generation of empowered thinkers, doers, and builders.
